# Persona Mapping

| Persona | Perspective | Landing page | Detail pages | Primary governed products |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Executive | Executive | Executive | Group overview, airport comparison | executive scorecard, flight/energy/commercial/incident KPIs |
| Airport manager | Airport | Airport | airport comparison, passenger flow, baggage, incidents, CX | airport, flow, baggage, incident/CX KPIs |
| Airline station manager | Airline | Airline | flight performance, turnaround, baggage | airline route, flight, baggage KPIs |
| Operations control | Operations | Operations | turnaround control, passenger flow, gates/stands, workforce | flight, flow, baggage, workforce KPIs |
| Maintenance | Maintenance | Maintenance | maintenance/assets, energy | maintenance, asset reliability, energy KPIs |
| Commercial/retail | Commercial | Commercial | retail/revenue, customer experience | commercial and incident/CX KPIs |
| IT/data operations | IT | IT | data quality/platform operations | IT service health, lineage, deployment, agent context |

Every page displays or carries the annotation `Synthetic demonstration - advisory only`. Airport and Operations landing pages plus passenger/baggage pages use packaged WGS84 GeoJSON. Public airport centroids are reference data; all detailed geometry and operational values are synthetic.
