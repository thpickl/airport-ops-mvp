# Privacy and Responsible AI

- Purpose limitation: stakeholder demonstration of governed analytics only.
- Data minimization: public airport anchor facts plus synthetic operational fields required for the scenario.
- Identity: deterministic pseudonyms; no real passenger or employee identity.
- Consent: synthetic opt-in flags for cohort recommendations.
- Cohorts: minimum Data Agent output cohort is 10.
- Recommendations: cohort based, protected-characteristic-free, advisory, and subject to explicit human review.
- Provenance: source objects, data-as-of, freshness, confidence, and quality warnings are mandatory.
- Prompt safety: source allow-listing, read-only tools, instruction-override refusal, and no action tools.
- Decision audit: recommendation evidence, approval/rejection/deferral, and rationale are retained in synthetic workflow records.
- Regulatory boundary: generated reports are preparation aids, never official submissions or legal conclusions.
- Operational boundary: no connectors or commands to ATC, AODB, BHS, BMS, aircraft, equipment, operational technology, or workforce systems.

The fictional scenario references GDPR, EU AI Act, EASA-related demonstration requirements, national civil-aviation reporting, sector directives, and Jordan-specific distinctions only as governance context. The package is not legal advice or compliance certification.
